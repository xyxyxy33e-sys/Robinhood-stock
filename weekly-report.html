Notes · week 2

Memory did the damage
Sandisk −7.3%, Applied Materials −5.9%, Micron −4.1%, Lam −2.5%, Seagate −2.3%. The storage-and-equipment complex is about 32% of the book, and it moved together. ExxonMobil's −5.6% was a separate, energy-specific decline. NVIDIA and Alphabet were the only names to finish higher.
Why we lagged the fund
Not tracking error in the usual sense — the weights are right. It is leverage-by-concentration: holding SPMO's top fifteen at 100% instead of 63% amplifies whatever those fifteen do, up and down. In a week where the fund's largest names underperformed its smaller ones, that costs. The ten-year study assumed and measured this behaviour.
Reconstitution is coming
The S&P Momentum index reconstitutes on the third Friday of September — 18 September 2026, effective the next trading day. That is the one Friday of this half-year where the roster can change materially, and the first rebalance likely to require more than two trades. Everything before it should stay quiet.
What this is not
Two weeks is still noise. Down 5.5% since inception is unpleasant but unremarkable for a concentrated momentum sleeve; the ten-year, twenty-cohort study that justifies this strategy contains far worse fortnights inside periods that ended well ahead.

What this is not
One week is noise. The strategy's case rests on a ten-year, twenty-cohort study, and even that concluded it beats a Nasdaq index fund by a thin margin earned through shallower drawdowns rather than higher returns. A −3.8% opening week is well inside normal variation.
