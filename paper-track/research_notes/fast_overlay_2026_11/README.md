# Fast re-entry overlay (20/100): where the proxy drawdown comes from, which half earns, can it be made safer (2026-11-17)

**Research only. Nothing adopted, nothing in the repo edited, committed or pushed.** `PLAN.md` was written before any arm was run (reading rule fixed there); `run.py` -> `run.log`
(44 s) and `tables.md` (the tables pasted below). Engine = `paper-track/harness_live.py` pieces (live design, L1 off). Checks inside `run.py`: FULL equals `HL.run_live` to 0.0 on real / p01 / p10;
OFF_audit reproduces the 2026-11-17 audit's R02 OFF arm (real 47.8 / 2.065 / -16.5, proxy 30.1 / 1.417 / -18.6); the copied weight function equals `MC.target_weights` to 0.0.
Windows: real = 2015-11-03.., p01 = proxy 2001-01-03.., p10 = proxy 2010-01-05.. (all to 2026-11-16).

**Framing.** The overlay was adopted 2026-09-06 on overlapping data (26-year proxy, SPMO-era real), and trim v2, the D ladder/gate, the vol-target estimator, the A 40/60 split, the cap and the sleeve
were all tuned with it ON. OFF is therefore partly a hybrid that never existed, and nothing here is out-of-sample. (The audit's specific hybrid, trim held votes computed on the overlay's A spells,
turns out not to matter: OFF_audit and a clean OFF, held votes recomputed on macro A spells, differ by 0.1 pp CAGR and 0.001 Sharpe and have identical max DD.)

## Verdict (reading rule in PLAN.md)
* **Keep FULL unchanged.** None of the 20 alternatives satisfies the six conditions (table "Reading rule" below); the only arm with a 2 pp shallower p01 max DD (LR100, +3.0 pp) gives the overlay's return and Sharpe back (Sharpe -0.041 / -0.060 / -0.034 on real / p01 / p10, below its own matched de-lever).
* **Do not turn the overlay off.** FULL de-levered to OFF's average gross has a higher Sharpe than OFF on all three windows (OFF 2.064 / 1.417 / 1.688 vs 2.118 / 1.496 / 1.753; OFF wins 0 of 3, rule needs 2). Same for OFF_audit.
* The deeper proxy drawdown is real and has a clear mechanism (below): the BC->A half buys the A row at the top of bear-market rallies (Aug 2008, Aug 2022, Mar 2002, Sep 2004). It is not a 2008-only accident, and it is not one the overlay's own variants cure without losing the return.

## Q1. Where does the deeper proxy drawdown come from?
Top 5 drawdown episodes on p01 (peak -> trough -> recovery; depth):

| FULL | OFF (clean; OFF_audit identical depths) |
|---|---|
| **-22.1%** 2008-08-14 -> 2009-03-09 -> 2009-07-15 | -18.6% 2004-12-14 -> 2005-03-14 -> 2006-01-11 |
| -20.1% 2022-08-15 -> 2023-01-19 -> 2023-03-13 | -18.3% 2007-07-19 -> 2007-08-16 -> 2007-09-26 |
| -18.6% 2004-12-14 -> 2005-03-14 -> 2005-11-25 | -18.2% 2008-08-14 -> 2009-03-09 -> 2009-07-14 |
| -18.3% 2007-07-19 -> 2007-08-16 -> 2007-09-26 | -17.0% 2025-02-19 -> 2025-03-04 -> 2025-07-03 |
| -17.0% 2025-02-19 -> 2025-03-04 -> 2025-06-04 | -16.4% 2011-04-27 -> 2011-08-08 -> 2012-02-03 |

* The 3.5 pp gap (-22.1 vs -18.6) is FULL's 2008 episode against OFF's 2004-05 episode (which the overlay does not touch: no overlay spell in it). Over the same 2008 days FULL is -22.1% and OFF -18.2%.
* **Switches in the 2008 window.** BC->A, macro C / fast B, 2008-08-08..08-22 (11 sessions, QQQ -1.8%, FULL book -3.9% vs OFF -0.5%, -3.51 pp log; -5.65 pp of it falls after the 08-14 peak). Then F->C, macro F / fast C: 2008-10-30..11-05 (QQQ -6.9%, -1.33 pp), 12-18..12-26 (-0.72); offset by 12-05..12-15 (+1.31) and 12-31 (+1.25). Net inside the window -5.0 pp log.
* **The peak is the overlay's own high**: FULL's NAV was 1.439x OFF's on 2008-08-14 (the spring BC->A spell 2008-04-21..06-06 alone added +8.4 pp) and still 1.369x OFF's at the 2009-03-09 trough; 2008 calendar year FULL +0.1% vs OFF -4.4%. So FULL ends the episode ahead in dollars but deeper in percent.
* **Spread over several bear markets, not one episode.** Max DD inside sub-periods, FULL vs OFF: 2001-03 -13.8 vs -10.2; 2007-09 -22.1 vs -18.3; 2022 -19.6 vs -13.2 (2022-08-15 -> 2023-01-19: FULL -20.1% vs OFF -14.5%, spell BC->A 2022-08-03..08-19 C/B -5.64 pp inside the window, F->C 2022-10-21..11-01 -1.93). Max DD only reports the worst of them (2008). Identical elsewhere (2011, 2018, 2020, 2025-26). On the real window max DD is the same (-16.5%, a 2025 episode the overlay never touches); real 2022 is +2.2% FULL vs +5.2% OFF.
* All 68 overlay spells on p01 (29 BC->A, 467 sessions, +62.7 pp log vs OFF; 39 F->C, 216 sessions, +13.8 pp; total +77.5 pp = FULL NAV 2.17x OFF's). Worst spells: 2018-12-03..06 F->C (-7.10 pp, QQQ -6.4%), 2008-08-08..22 BC->A (-3.51), 2022-05-27..06-09 F->C (-3.49), 2001-08-01..07 F->C (-3.19), 2006-06-29..07-06 F->C (-3.04), 2022-08-03..19 BC->A (-2.80), 2002-03-08..12 BC->A (-2.59). Worst 5 = -20.3 pp, 39% of all negative spells. Best: 2023-01-20..02-17 BC->A +11.92, 2010-09-09..27 +9.17, 2019-02-05..04-01 +8.73, 2008-04-21..06-06 +8.43. BC->A spells positive 17 / negative 12, F->C 26 / 13.
* Pattern: macro C with fast B (or B/B) holds the A row (2.0x after cap) through a bear-market rally top; on roll-over the A row loses ~2x QQQ's move while OFF sits at 1.0x (C) or 0.5x (B). The "adoption evidence" max DD (-34.8% vs -36.4%, old live design, no cap, no vol-target) is a different book; with the 2.0x cap, vol target and ladder, F->C / BC->A at the 2008 and 2022 rally tops is a net cost to the worst drawdown. (I did not re-run the adoption-era design.)

| F_ONLY (F -> C) | -0.1 pp / -0.002 / 0.0 | +0.7 pp / +0.016 / 0.0 | +0.5 pp / +0.012 / 0.0 |
| FULL | +3.3 pp / +0.036 / 0.0 | +4.0 pp / +0.063 / -3.6 pp | +3.6 pp / +0.052 / -3.0 pp |

BC->A earns nearly all of the return (+62.7 of +77.5 pp log on p01) **and** takes all of the max-DD cost; F->C is nearly free on drawdown (F_ONLY has the same max DD as OFF on all three windows) but earns little (+0.7 pp CAGR p01, nothing on real) and has 13 losing spells including the worst single one (2018-12). Against its own matched de-lever BC_ONLY is -0.002 / -0.018 / -0.014 Sharpe (real / p01 / p10) and F_ONLY -0.053 / -0.060 / -0.050: neither half alone beats simply holding less of FULL; the whole overlay is above OFF's matched de-lever (see Verdict). F->C fires essentially only when the fast read is C (price above the 20-day, below the 100-day): F_A and F_AB behave like BC_ONLY (real and p10 identical; p01 within 0.02 Sharpe).

## Q3. Variants (arms and results in Tables 1-2; 14 pre-registered + OFF_audit + 6 neighbours)
Nothing improves p01 max DD by 2 pp without losing Sharpe:
* **LR100** (B/C hold 100% SPMO): p01 max DD -19.1% (+3.0 pp), but CAGR 47.8 / 30.8 / 41.3 (that is OFF's return) and Sharpe -0.041 / -0.060 / -0.034 vs FULL. LR75 +0.8 pp DD, Sharpe -0.032 / -0.066 / -0.027. LR50 *deeper* (-23.5%): half TQQQ at a rally top is the same risk and less return. All three lose to their matched de-lever on p01.
* **F->C only on fast A/B (F_AB, F_A):** no change to the BC half, so p01 max DD -22.7% (-0.5 pp), Sharpe -0.019 / -0.015 p01.
* **Confirmation (CONF2, CONF3):** p01 max DD -24.0% / -23.5% (deeper: the confirmation delays the entry in good rallies but the bad rally tops stay), Sharpe -0.010 / -0.014 p01; real -0.018 / +0.009 (noise).
* **Windows:** W20_80 -24.4% / -0.029 p01, W30_100 -23.3% / -0.038, W30_150 (old micro pair) -23.6% / -0.080 (p10 -0.072, bootstrap P(dSh<=0) 1.00 p01): every neighbour of 20/100 is worse on Sharpe on all windows; 20/100 sits on or near a local best, as STRATEGY.md claimed. (On real the 2022 year costs W20_80 -3.5 pp vs FULL.)
* **Stress filters:** V25 (overlay off while 30-day QQQ vol > 25%): Sharpe +0.031 / +0.014 / +0.042, CAGR 51.3 / 33.4 / 44.3, **but p01 max DD -22.7% (0.5 pp deeper)**; V30 +0.014 / +0.002 / +0.013; V40 +0.001 / -0.018 / +0.001. The 30-day vol sat at 24-28% at the 2008-08-08 / 2022-08-03 spell entries (25.3% / 27.6%; 37.7% at 2002-03-08, 19.3% at 2004-09-14), so a 25% threshold flickers around those spells and a 30-40% threshold does not trip: the filter does not cure the drawdown it was meant for. V25 behaves like OFF in the high-vol years (2001 -5.4, 2009 -6.7, 2020 -6.4 pp vs FULL) and like FULL in calm years; it gains in 2018 (+12.6 pp) and 2022 (+5.1), so its Sharpe lead comes from a few years. VIX > VIX3M (VT100, VT95, VT105): changes nothing before 2009-09-18 (the harness VIX3M start) and almost nothing after (Sharpe within 0.012 of FULL on every window, p01 DD identical); no value.
* V25 is a Sharpe-only lead, not a drawdown fix; bootstrap P(dSh<=0) 0.28 real / 0.34 p01 / 0.10 p10, and it is the best of three thresholds I looked at after the fact: treat as noise-level and not proposed.
