    Analytics are computed locally from daily OHLCV bars: SMA 20/50/200, RSI 14, MACD 12/26/9, ATR 14, Bollinger 20×2, OBV, realized volatility.
    Methodology: quality score, entry range, confidence

    Quality score (0–100) = trend health 30 (price vs SMA 20/50/200, SMA 50 slope) + momentum 20 (RSI band, MACD histogram) + volume & liquidity 15 (up/down-day volume ratio, dollar volume) + volatility regime 15 (ATR% and Bollinger-width percentile — calmer is better) + risk geometry 10 (drawdown from 52-week high) + valuation 10 (P/E sanity; neutral where N/A).

    Entry range is a pullback zone: from max(support, price − 1.75×ATR) up to price − 0.25×ATR, where support is the higher of the lower Bollinger band and the nearer of SMA 20/50. It is a patience band under the current price, not a prediction.

    Confidence counts independent bullish signals (price > SMA 20, price > SMA 50, SMA 50 > SMA 200, RSI 40–70, MACD histogram positive, MACD histogram rising, OBV rising — plus, when options data is present, implied vol below 1.5× realized and put/call open interest below 1.2): ≥70% aligned is high, ≥43% medium, else low.

    Options metrics (via Public.com) come from the option chain nearest 30 days out: ATM implied volatility averages the at-the-money call and put IV; the expected move is the ATM straddle mid-price; 25Δ skew is the 25-delta put IV minus the 25-delta call IV (higher = more downside fear); highest-OI strikes often act as magnets/pins near expiry.
